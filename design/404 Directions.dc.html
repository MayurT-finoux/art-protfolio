1 404 page: two directions

Same game, same nav and footer, two looks. The world starts as a pencil sketch, gets inked at 220 and painted at 600. Every frame is live: hover a frame, then press space (or click / tap). Playing frames run on autopilot until you take over. Character, obstacles and backgrounds are doodle placeholders for your own art.
